What to look for in a stand case

  • The exact model. Cases for similar-looking phones are often not interchangeable, so match the full name.
  • Stand style. Kickstands that fold flat are slim. Ring stands hold the phone at an angle, and rotating ones offer more positions.
  • MagSafe or magnets. If you use magnetic chargers, wallets or mounts, pick a case that says it's compatible.
  • Camera protection. A raised lip around the camera helps protect the lenses when the phone is face-down.
  • Heat and charging. Thick cases can trap heat. If your phone feels hot while charging, take the case off. See our slow charging guide.
  • Returns. A bad fit is common with cases, so buy from a seller with an easy return policy.

FAQ

Does a stand case work with wireless charging?

Often yes, but a thick case or a metal part in the stand can slow it or stop it. Look for listings that say they support wireless or MagSafe charging.

Will a case fit if I upgrade my phone?

No. Cases are made for a specific model, so you'll need a new one.

Will the price change?

Yes. Prices on Amazon move often, so check the live price before you buy.
