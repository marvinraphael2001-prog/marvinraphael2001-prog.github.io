How to choose a power bank

  • Capacity (mAh). Most phone batteries are roughly 3,000 to 5,000mAh. A power bank loses some power as heat when it charges, so a 5,000mAh one gives you about one partial charge, and a 25,000mAh one several.
  • Output (watts). 20W or more is good for fast phone charging. A laptop needs much more, so check your laptop's charger rating before you buy.
  • Size and weight. Small ones fit in a pocket. Large ones are better for a bag or a trip.
  • Wired or wireless. Wired charging is faster and wastes less power. Wireless is more convenient.
  • Safety. Buy from known brands with plenty of recent reviews, and look for safety certification on the listing. Stop using any power bank that swells, gets very hot or smells odd.
  • Flying. Most airlines require power banks in carry-on bags, not checked luggage, and limit large ones. Check your airline's rules before a trip, especially for high-capacity models.

FAQ

How many times can a power bank charge my phone?

It depends on the capacity and your phone's battery. As a rough guide, a 5,000mAh power bank gives about one partial charge, and a 25,000mAh one gives several. Real results are lower than the number on the label.

Can a power bank charge a laptop?

Only some can. It needs enough power output (watts) and a USB-C port that supports Power Delivery. Check what your laptop needs first.
