What to check before you buy

  • USB-C and Power Delivery (PD). This is what enables fast charging on modern iPhones and most Android phones.
  • Wattage. 20W is enough for most phones. 30W is better if you also charge tablets or want future-proofing.
  • Your cable. A fast charger with a worn or cheap cable will still charge slowly. Use a USB-C cable rated for fast charging.
  • A trusted brand. Avoid unbranded chargers with very low prices, since poor ones can overheat.
  • Samsung users: look for PPS support if you want the fastest speeds. Check your phone's specs for the exact figure.

FAQ

Do I need a 30W charger for my phone?

Not necessarily. Many phones fast-charge fine with 20W. A 30W charger gives you extra headroom and works for tablets too.

Does the charger come with a cable?

Often not. Check the listing, and buy a good USB-C cable if you don't have one.

Will the price change?

Yes. Prices on Amazon move often, so check the live price before you buy.
