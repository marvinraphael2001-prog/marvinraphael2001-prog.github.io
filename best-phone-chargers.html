What to check before you buy

  • USB-C and Power Delivery (PD). This is what enables fast charging on modern iPhones and most Android phones.
  • Wattage. 20W is enough for most phones. Higher wattage helps with tablets and laptops.
  • Shared power. On multi-port chargers, each port gets less power when several are in use.
  • Your cable. A fast charger with a worn or cheap cable will still charge slowly.
  • Safety marks and reviews. Prefer chargers with safety certification (such as UL or ETL) and a good number of recent reviews. Avoid very cheap unbranded chargers, since poor ones can overheat.
  • Return policy. If a charger gets hot or doesn't work, return it and stop using it.

FAQ

Do I need a very high wattage charger for my phone?

Not usually. Phones only draw the power they can handle, so a higher-wattage charger won't hurt them. It just gives you extra headroom for tablets and laptops.

Do these chargers come with a cable?

Often not. Check each listing, and use a good USB-C or Lightning cable.

Will the price change?

Yes. Prices on Amazon move often, so check the live price before you buy.
