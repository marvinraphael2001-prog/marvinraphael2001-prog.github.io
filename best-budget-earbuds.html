What to look for in budget earbuds

  • Bluetooth 5.2 or newer for stable connections and better battery efficiency.
  • Battery life with the case, not just per charge. Listing claims are best-case figures.
  • Water resistance if you'll use them for workouts.
  • Noise cancelling only if you commute or travel. It's rare under $30.
  • Return policy. With lesser-known brands, buy where returns are easy.

FAQ

Are cheap earbuds worth it?

Yes, for most people. Budget models now cover everyday listening, calls and workouts well. You mainly give up premium sound tuning and top-tier noise cancelling.

Do any of these have noise cancelling?

The Soundcore P30i has active noise cancelling. The two LED-display models advertise noise reduction for calls. The JLab and the clip-on pair do not cancel noise.
