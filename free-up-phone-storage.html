How to Free Up Storage on Any Phone

Seeing "Storage Almost Full" is annoying, and it can slow your phone down and stop photos and updates from saving. The good news is you can usually clear several gigabytes in about 10 minutes without deleting anything you care about. These steps work on both iPhone and Android.

Before you start: Back up your photos and important files first, either to the cloud or to a computer. That way nothing is lost if you delete the wrong thing.

1. See what's using the space

On iPhone, open Settings → General → iPhone Storage. On Android, open Settings → Storage (the exact name varies a little by brand). You'll see a breakdown by category and by app, so you know where to start.

2. Delete or offload apps you don't use

Old apps and games can take up gigabytes. Remove anything you haven't opened in months. On iPhone, Offload App removes the app but keeps its data, so reinstalling brings everything back. On Android, Google Play shows apps by last-used date, which makes cleanup easy.

3. Back up and clear photos and videos

Photos and videos are usually the biggest space hog, especially 4K video. Back them up to iCloud, Google Photos, or a computer, then remove the local copies. On iPhone, turn on Optimize
