How to Make Your Phone Battery Last Longer (7 Easy Tips)

If your phone is dead by mid-afternoon, you may not need a new one. A few settings and habits can add real hours to your day, and they're all free. Menu names differ a little between iPhone, Samsung, Pixel, and other Android phones, but each tip below works on all of them.

1. Check what's actually draining your battery

Open Settings → Battery. Your phone lists which apps use the most power. If an app you barely use sits near the top, restrict its background activity or uninstall it.

2. Lower your screen brightness

The screen is one of the biggest battery users. Turn on auto-brightness, and drop the level manually when you're indoors. Also shorten the screen timeout to 30 seconds or one minute so it doesn't stay lit when you put the phone down.

3. Use dark mode

On phones with OLED screens, which includes most recent iPhones and many Samsung and Pixel models, dark mode lights fewer pixels and can save battery. On older LCD screens the difference is small, but it's easier on the eyes at night.

4. Turn on low power or battery saver mode

This mode cuts background activity, reduces animations, and limits some features. Switch it on when you're below about 30% and know you won't be near a charger for a while. You can also set it to start automatically on most Android phones.

5. Limit background refresh, location, and notifications

Many apps keep refreshing and tracking you even when closed. In your settings, set location access to "While using the app" for most apps, turn off background app refresh for ones you don't need, and mute notifications from apps that spam you. Each one wakes the screen and uses data.

6. Watch your signal

When signal is weak, your phone works harder to stay connected and drains faster. Use Wi-Fi when it's available. If you're somewhere with poor 5G coverage, switching to 4G/LTE can help. Airplane mode is useful in places with no signal at all.

7. Keep your phone cool

Heat is hard on batteries. Avoid leaving your phone in a hot car, in direct sun, or under a pillow while it charges. If it feels hot, take off the case and let it cool down. Over time, regular heat makes the battery wear out faster.

If your battery still drains fast

If you've tried all seven tips and the battery still runs out quickly, the battery itself may be worn. On iPhone, check Settings → Battery → Battery Health. Many Android phones show similar information in Settings. A battery replacement from the maker or a trusted repair shop is usually much cheaper than a new phone. Slow charging can also be a sign of a worn cable or weak charger, so see our guide to fixing slow charging.

Quick recap

Find the apps draining your battery, lower the brightness, use dark mode, turn on battery saver, limit background activity, watch your signal, and keep your phone cool.
